Our Story

Built on Infrastructure.
Driven by People.

Executive Fiber was founded on one conviction: that reliable, fast internet should not be a luxury. We built our own network to prove it.

Mission

Connecting Communities, One Fiber at a Time

We started Executive Fiber because we saw a gap: homes and businesses across the country were underserved by legacy providers who over-promised and under-delivered.

So we did something different. We laid our own fiber, built our own network, and hired a local team that knows this region inside out. Today we serve hundreds of clients across the nation.

Our mission is simple: bring enterprise-grade connectivity to every home and business that needs it, with transparent pricing and support that actually shows up.

Timeline

How We Got Here

From a single fiber run to a regional network — the Executive Fiber journey.

2019

Founded in Kitengela

Executive Fiber is established with a small team and a single goal: provide honest, fast internet to underserved areas of the country.

2020

First 50 Clients

We hit our first 50 residential clients and prove the model works — consistent speeds, fair pricing, and local support that responds within hours.

2021

Business Broadband Launch

We expand into dedicated business broadband with SLA guarantees, attracting schools, hotels, and SMEs across every region.

2023

Hotspot & Managed WiFi

We launch our managed hotspot solution, enabling hotels, estates and commercial landlords to offer branded WiFi with full billing control.

2025

500 Mbps Infrastructure

Major fiber backbone upgrade — we now deliver up to 500 Mbps to enterprise clients, with redundant links for maximum reliability.

2026

500+ Clients & Growing

We serve over 500 active homes and businesses, with ongoing expansion into new estates and neighboring towns.
